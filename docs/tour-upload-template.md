- [ ] All 8 sections filled

- [ ] Itinerary has realistic timing (travel time between stops accounted for)
- [ ] Inclusions/exclusions match the pricing
- [ ] Cancellation policy defined
- [ ] EN + KO descriptions ready
- [ ] At least 1 customer review or testimonial
